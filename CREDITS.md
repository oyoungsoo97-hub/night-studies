# Night Studies credits

The platform, transit print, carriage, hallway, figure and diner are original generated visual assets created for this collection. The diners and rail scenes are fictional. The audio instruments and playback do not use recorded performances.

## Hand tracking

The optional camera interaction uses Google's MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.14 browser runtime and its WebAssembly files. Bundled license and third-party notices are in [LICENSE.txt](docs/assets/hands/LICENSE.txt). [MediaPipe](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe) · [JavaScript package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mediapipe/tasks-vision/v/0.10.14)

The hand landmarker model is downloaded from Google's published version 1 distribution on first use. SHA-256 is `fbc2a30080c3c557093b5ddfc334698132eb341044ccee322ccf8bcf3607cde1`. [Hand Landmarker documentation](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker)
